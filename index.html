04 / EXPERIENCE

My first step into a workplace.

MAY — JUNE 2026

Apollo Hospital, Rourkela

Marketing Intern

A

My first internship experience was at Apollo Hospital, Rourkela. During the internship, I got practical exposure to healthcare marketing and how a large hospital operates.

I learned about different hospital departments and functions, studied the market and competitors such as Max Hospital, Hitech, JP and CWS, and worked on marketing-related activities including SWOT analysis, AIDA analysis, content calendar planning and promotional poster ideas.

I also explored Digital Marketing, Trade Marketing, Corporate Marketing and Community Marketing and worked on understanding community outreach activities and free medical camps.

This experience helped me connect what I was learning in BBA with a real business environment and gave me more confidence in myself.
